The 18 suppliers that drive the next 25-40 per cent of scope 3 emissions should be a focus for UNICEF to engage more deeply, as these suppliers do not yet have SBTs. UNICEF can follow best practice by holding industry consultations with suppliers to share details about requirements, answer supplier questions, and share materials to support them in setting SBTs. One-to-one meetings may also provide guidance on developing an emission footprint, target-setting and disclosure/reporting, as well as discussing specific emissions abatement opportunities.
