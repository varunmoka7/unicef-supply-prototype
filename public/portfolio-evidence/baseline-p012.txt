Combined with a supplier analysis, it is evident that emissions are concentrated in a relatively
small number of key suppliers – 30 highest-emitting suppliers (in 2019) account for 80-90 per cent
of UNICEF SD’s scope 3 emissions. These high emitting suppliers are spread across vaccines,
international freight, nutrition, LLINs and cold chain equipment. It will be critical to engage with such
suppliers, set supplier targets, share education and joint supplier solutions, as well as consider
supplier incentives. This is discussed further in section 5.
